⚡ About Grameen Light

A Smart Village Streetlight Monitoring System designed to help citizens report fused or power-wasting streetlights quickly and efficiently.

🚨 Problem Statement

Streetlights in villages often remain ON during the day due to manual error, wasting electricity. Sometimes fused lights remain OFF for weeks because the Panchayat office is unaware of the issue.

🌍 Vision

Grameen Light is a citizen-led streetlight audit platform. Villagers can report streetlight issues instantly, creating a digital bridge between residents and local authorities.

The goal is to ensure safer nights, better maintenance, and zero unnecessary electricity wastage.

📱 App Usage & User Flow

🗺 Pole Map

View all streetlight poles across the village.

⚡ Quick Report

Report Working, Fused, or Burning During Day status instantly.

🛠 Repair Tracker

Track complaint progress from Pending to Fixed.

🌱 Energy Goal

Reduce energy wastage through community participation.

💻 Technical Implementation

🔥 Real-Time Sync

Firebase can sync reports instantly across the village dashboard.

🎨 Modern UI

Dark/Light themes represent night/day audit modes.

🗄 Database

Store complaint and pole data securely for long-term records.

🌟 Impact Goals

  • Improve public safety during nighttime.
  • Reduce electricity wastage in villages.
  • Enable smart village infrastructure using citizen participation.
  • Create faster communication between villagers and Panchayat offices.

✅ Success Criteria

  • Complaint IDs generated for every report.
  • Color-coded pole statuses visible clearly.
  • Simple one-tap reporting experience.
  • Repair progress visible to all villagers.
